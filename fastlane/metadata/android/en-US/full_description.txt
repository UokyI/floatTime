Floating Countdown is a minimal, glassy floating countdown timer that works as an always-on-top floating bubble on your screen.

Features:
* A circular floating icon that you can drag anywhere on the screen
* Long-press the bubble to set a task name and a countdown in minutes
* After starting, the top half of the bubble shows the first two characters of the task name while the bottom half shows a seconds-level countdown (e.g. 4:59)
* Vibrates when the countdown finishes
* Choose your own idle/active colors and the bubble diameter
* Chinese / English in-app language switch

Color scheme: peach RGB(251,204,199) / mint RGB(149,197,172). A liquid/glass dynamic UI.
